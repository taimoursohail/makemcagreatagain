Everything ISO Partners Need to Know

Straight answers to questions we get asked most. If yours is not here use chat widget or shoot us an email.

We actively fund B through D paper. We are not a fund that only chases A-grade cherry picks. If revenue story is clean and cash flow supports the deal we will look at it and give you a real reason if we pass.

Most complete files get a decision within 4–6 business hours of submission same day when possible. We do not do the "3 business day" fake SLA thing. If we say we will get back to you we will with an actual answer not a request for 7 more docs.

We look at true business operating revenue: merchant processing deposits ACH customer payments and verifiable business income. Excluded: Zelle Cash App inter-account transfers loan proceeds or any deposits that are not clearly from sale of goods/services.

Just email the file directly to brandon@makemcagreatagain.com with completed app and 3 months' statements. No login no mandatory fields no file getting lost in a portal black hole.

Repeat deals and renewals from existing merchants get fast-tracked. If merchant has been making payments cleanly and updated statements look good we can often issue a renewal offer same-day.
