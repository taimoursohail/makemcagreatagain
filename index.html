FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything ISO Partners Need to Know

Straight answers to the questions we get asked most. If yours isn't here, use the chat widget or shoot us an email.

We actively fund B through D paper. We're not a fund that only chases A-grade cherry picks. If the revenue story is clean and the cash flow supports the deal, we'll look at it — and give you a real reason if we pass.

Most complete files get a decision within 4–6 business hours of submission, same day when possible. We don't do the "3 business day" fake SLA thing. If we say we'll get back to you, we will — with an actual answer, not a request for 7 more docs.

We look at true business operating revenue — merchant processing deposits, ACH customer payments, and verifiable business income. Excluded: Zelle, Cash App, inter-account transfers, loan proceeds, or any deposits that aren't clearly from the sale of goods/services.

Just email the file directly to brandon@makemcagreatagain.com with a completed app and 3 months' statements. No login, no mandatory fields, no file getting lost in a portal black hole.

Repeat deals and renewals from existing merchants get fast-tracked. If the merchant has been making payments cleanly and the updated statements look good, we can often issue a renewal offer same-day.

Click "Request ISO Agreement" in the header form above, fill in your details, and the agreement goes out via Proposify for e-signature — tracked and automated. Once signed, we'll schedule an onboarding call, share the full underwriting guideline deck, and get your payout setup locked in.
